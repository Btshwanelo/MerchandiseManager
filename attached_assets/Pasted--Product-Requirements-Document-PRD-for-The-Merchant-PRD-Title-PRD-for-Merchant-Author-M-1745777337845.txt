## One Pager

### Overview
The Merchant is an inventory management tool designed for third-party merchandisers working on behalf of FMCG brands. This tool streamlines stocktaking processes, manages merchandising promotions, and documents competitor activities, enabling real-time data collection and analysis to optimize inventory management.

### Problem
Current inventory management processes for FMCG brands lack efficiency and real-time data capture. Merchandisers face challenges in accurately tracking stock levels, documenting promotions, and providing insights on competitor activities. These inefficiencies lead to missed opportunities for sales and promotional effectiveness, ultimately affecting brand performance in retail environments.

### Objectives
-  **Objective 1:** Streamline the stocktaking process to ensure accurate inventory levels are captured.
-  **Objective 2:** Enhance visibility of promotional activities through real-time data collection.
-  **Objective 3:** Provide actionable insights on competitor promotions to inform strategic decisions.

### Constraints
-  **Constraint 1:** Limited integration capabilities with existing inventory systems.
-  **Constraint 2:** Time constraints for development and deployment before peak retail seasons.
-  **Constraint 3:** Dependence on the accuracy of data provided by merchandisers.

### Persona
-  **Key Persona:** Third-party merchandisers working for FMCG brands.

---

### Use Cases
-  **Scenario 1:** A merchandiser arrives at a store to conduct a stocktake, using the app to record stock levels and take shelf images.
-  **Scenario 2:** The merchandiser documents current promotions and competitor activities, uploading images and details through the app.
-  **Scenario 3:** The merchandiser reviews product placement guidelines and promotional deals within the app to optimize shelf presentation.

## PRD

### Features In
1. **Stock Take/Availability**
   - Pre-upload product information for easy selection.
   - Summary of stock availability with commentary textbox.
   - Ability to take up to 5 shelf pictures per store.

2. **Merchandising/Promotions**
   - Drop-down for product options and a box to insert price.
   - Upload feature for current promotional images.

3. **Competitor Merchandising/Promotions**
   - Textboxes for brand and product description, promotional price.
   - Upload feature for competitor promotional images.

4. **Flows Tab**
   - Standard picture of product shelf order provided in PowerPoint format.

5. **Product Sheet Tab**
   - PDF showcasing available products.

6. **List Price Tab**
   - Upload feature for list prices in PDF or Excel format.

7. **Deals Tab**
   - Upload feature for promotional deals and discounts in Excel format.

8. **Orders Tab**
   - Capture images of orders placed by merchandisers.

### Features Out
1. **Real-time inventory updates** - Due to integration limitations with existing systems.
2. **Automated competitor analysis** - Complexity in developing AI-driven insights.
3. **Multi-store management** - Focus on individual store stocktaking for initial rollout.
